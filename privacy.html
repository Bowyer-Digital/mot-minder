MOT Minder

Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

MOT Minder (“the app”) is published by Bowyer Digital (“we”). We built it to keep your information on your phone. This policy explains the small amount of data that leaves your device and why.

Data stored on your device

The vehicles you add, their details and history, nicknames, your own reminders and your settings are stored only on your iPhone. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Vehicle lookups

When you add or refresh a vehicle, the app sends its registration number to our lookup service, hosted by Supabase in the United Kingdom (London region). The service asks the DVLA Vehicle Enquiry Service and the DVSA MOT History service for that vehicle’s details and returns them to your phone. We do not store the registration or the vehicle details. To prevent abuse we record only a random, anonymous app identifier and the time of each lookup, and delete these records after two days. The anonymous identifier is not linked to your name, email or Apple ID.

The DVLA and DVSA receive the registration number as part of each lookup, under their own terms and privacy notices.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple. We use RevenueCat to confirm whether you have Pro. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier and your purchase receipts from Apple; it does not receive your name or email. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Apple Calendar (optional)

If you turn on Apple Calendar, the app asks for calendar access so it can create its own “MOT Minder” calendar (stored wherever your calendars live, e.g. iCloud) and keep your due dates in it. It only adds, changes or removes events in that calendar, never reads or changes your other calendars, and nothing is sent to us.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your phone. No data is sent to send them.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • We never sell or share your data.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us for access to, or deletion of, any data we hold about you. We hold no vehicle data, and lookup records are deleted automatically within two days. Contact: support@bowyer.digital.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy we’ll update this page and the date above.
